About

PainterStorytellerAcrylic & impastoIdentityGrowth

The Ghor Brand is the studio of Ogor Opene, a Nigerian painter making contemporary African art about identity, growth and the human experience.

Acrylic, nylon and thick impasto on canvas. Figures caught mid-transformation: still forming, still questioning, still learning who they are beyond the expectations placed upon them.
